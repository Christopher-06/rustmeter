# Advanced Topics

Now that you have a firm grasp underneath the basics of RustMeter, it is time to plunge a bit deeper. 

In this Advanced Topics section, we peel back the layers to discuss underlying system characteristics that typically emerge as you start tracing heavier, more complex implementations. 

We will talk thoroughly about the unavoidable reality of profiling overhead, demonstrating exactly why certain things behave the way they do on physical chips, and layout strategies on minimizing your impact on target algorithms. 

Furthermore, we offer an extensive Troubleshooting hub designed to smoothly assist you past unexpected hurdles or complicated deadlock anomalies that can occasionally surface securely within Embedded Rust environments.
